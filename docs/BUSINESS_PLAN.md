## Executive Summary

### The Opportunity
Haazir addresses a ₹5,000+ crore market opportunity in educational technology, specifically targeting the attendance management and academic scheduling pain points that affect 1.5 million+ educational institutions in India.

### Our Solution
An AI-powered platform combining:
- **Dual-verification attendance** (QR + Face + GPS)
- **Intelligent timetable generation** (CSP + Gemini AI)
- **Unified analytics dashboard**

### Traction
- ✅ MVP deployed and functional
- ✅ Core AI features working
- ✅ Ready for pilot deployment

### Ask
- **Pilot Phase:** ₹5-10 lakhs for 6-month runway
- **Growth Phase:** ₹50 lakhs - ₹1 crore for 18-month expansion

## Pricing Model

### Pricing Philosophy
- **Value-based pricing:** Price reflects value delivered, not just cost
- **Land and expand:** Start with basic, upsell to premium
- **Annual commitment discount:** 20% off for annual payment
- **Volume discounts:** Larger institutions get better rates
